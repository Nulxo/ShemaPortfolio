Executive Functioning

Branding, Vector Illustrations, Print Design

Role: Brand Designer, Illustrator


I led a semester-long executive functioning workshop as in-house designer, directing branding, vector illustration, and digital and print production. The workshop raises awareness of neurological challenges neurodivergent individuals face daily, and I drew on personal experience to create relatable visuals and narratives that fostered connection and participation.

My process centered on building a brand identity that balanced accessibility, academic rigor, and innovation. This included sourcing executive dysfunction imagery, vectorizing and refining visuals, applying branding, designing layouts, and collaborating with copywriters.

Decorelle

Social Media, Branding, Vector Illustration

Role: Vector Illustrator, Photo Manipulation


Throughout Women's History Month, the Baltimore Mayor's Office of Communications spotlighted the sister duo behind Decorelle, celebrating their dedication to making luxury accessible to residents across Maryland and the DMV.

My approach centered on leveraging our vibrant, saturated brand palette to create striking contrast against Decorelle's warm, earthy tones. I developed minimalist yet eclectic vector illustrations that complemented their merchandise, using clipping masks and texture overlays to manipulate photography and achieve an editorial-inspired aesthetic.

Guilt-Free Bites

Editorial & Print Design

Role: Designer, Photographer


My passion for cuisine and cooking has inspired me to someday create a cookbook. Through this ongoing passion project, I envisioned a collection of hearty, comforting dishes that feel like home. Still in its early stages, this cucumber salad recipe is part of that larger vision.

The process began with preparing the dish firsthand. I then set up a simple DIY white-box studio for the photoshoot, capturing each ingredient separately before moving into post-production. Using adjustment layers, clipping masks, color manipulation, and vector enhancements, I added depth and clarity to showcase every
component authentically.

Buzz Campaign

UI/UX, Vector Illustrations

Role: UI/UX Designer


The Buzz Campaign is a community initiative highlighting outstanding staff, students, and faculty who uphold the University of Baltimore's welcoming environment. As part of its annual metrics reporting, I designed a dashboard visualizing the previous year's data, which was presented at the annual Eubie Awards, an end-of-year show celebrating pivotal individuals at the University.

Working within UBalt's brand system, including its color palette, typography, and imagery, I developed an intuitive, widget-based dashboard composed of modular UI components, each visualizing distinct data sets. I also built a sub-logo using vector illustrations to extend and support the initiative's brand identity.
