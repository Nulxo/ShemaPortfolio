A collection of videos showcasing my versatility as a storyteller, ranging from
short-form social media content to longer narrative pieces. Each video reflects a different purpose and highlights skills across directing, cinematography and editing.

A Walk Thru Remington

Camera, Director, Editor

Background

A visual exploration of the culture, lifestyle, and environment of Baltimore's Remington neighborhood. This piece was produced under Streetfront Digital, a personal project focused on documenting local communities, personal stories, and historical context to offer approachable insights for both newcomers and longtime residents.

Process

Shot run and gun with a dual perspective approach, blending first and third person vlog styles. This allowed me to toggle between the viewer's direct line of sight and an external view of how they would appear moving through the neighborhood, adding a layer of immersion and self awareness to the experience. Static compositions with natural movement establish the environment, letting the community's character emerge organically. I chose to let the subjects drive the visual dynamics rather than manipulating the footage, prioritizing authenticity over intervention.

Sumwalt Run: The Hidden Stream

Camera, Director, Editor

Background

During an exploration of Baltimore, we encountered the remnants of Sumwalt Run, a hidden stream with a noticeable presence threading through various neighborhoods. Our curiosity led us to research its history, revealing a fascinating architectural past and an evolving relationship with the city's landscape.

Process

This project required me to step into the roles of storyboarder and director for a historically focused, script heavy piece. My task was twofold: capture footage that faithfully followed the script while maintaining a compelling pace for the viewer. The shoot was intentionally loose and exploratory, allowing me to connect with the environment and translate it intuitively to screen. I recorded ambient sound and layered it throughout the edit to reinforce the educational narrative, and I incorporated motion design using Canva's animation tools to sustain engagement.

Celsius: One Sip, All In.

Camera, Director, Editor


Background

My undergraduate years were defined by late night productivity, fueled in large part by Celsius. When tasked with creating an ad for a subject of personal significance, I chose to center the project around the beverage that had become a staple of my routine. This decision grounded the work in genuine experience while allowing me to explore creative approaches to product storytelling.

Process

Demonstrating flexibility in media acquisition, I leveraged an everyday moment into a fully realized opening sequence. While consuming a Celsius can, I recognized the opportunity to capture a still image series on the spot, shooting the can from multiple angles. In post, I manipulated each photograph and repositioned the frames around a consistent anchor point, enabling seamless graphic matching across the match cut transitions. To further amplify the visual pacing, I recorded and synced custom Foley, transforming an impromptu photoshoot into a polished, rhythmically driven intro.

Rock the Block

Camera, Director, Editor

Background

Rock the Block, an annual event hosted by the University of Baltimore, celebrates the campus community, honors distinguished individuals, and reaffirms the university's commitment to community development and engagement.

Process

Shot run and gun under unpredictable conditions, which demanded acute situational awareness and rapid refocusing. My primary objective was to capture moving elements that would generate rhythmic, engaging patterns. In post production, I employed motion tracking to isolate and accentuate these trajectories, using the tracked data to drive dynamic reframes, anchored overlays, and heightened visual momentum throughout the edit.

Celsius: A Longer Version

Camera, Director, Editor

Background

This iteration of my Celsius advertisement expands on the brand's impact, showcasing its role across a range of professions and lifestyles.

The Decorelle Sisters

Editor

Background

During my time with the City of Baltimore, I produced a Women's History Month feature on Decorelle, a brand founded by two sisters dedicated to making interior design accessible and celebrating their cultural heritage within the Baltimore community.

Process

I crafted a video that emphasized the brand's modern aesthetic, glamour, and commitment to accessibility. Popular music was selected to drive a snappy, high energy presentation, with transitions precisely timed to the beat to maintain momentum. A-roll footage segment was included to humanize the brand, allowing the audience to connect faces with the founders and reinforcing the personal story behind the business.

Chasing Liberty

Director, Camera, Editor
Documentary, Video-Journalism

"Chasing Liberty" is a short film exploring the journeys of immigrants from around the world. Through personal interviews, they share what they left behind, how they adapted, and the dreams that brought them here. A reflection on loss, resilience, and the pursuit of something greater, how freedom, happiness, and hope carry across oceans.

Shelf Life

Director, Camera, Editor
Informational, Advertising, Educational

As a former UBalt student, I highlight its library as a heavily used, essential part of students’ daily routines. The goal is to showcase this secure, supportive space maintained by dedicated staff, student workers, and students committed to its upkeep. I conducted interviews with individuals in three roles: a librarian, a student worker, and a student user. This diverse selection emphasizes the library's importance and how each role helps sustain this vital resource.

The Bear

Editor, Motion Designer
Title Sequence, TV, Storytelling

Right after my trip to Chicago, which deepened my appreciation for the city, I discovered "The Bear," a show highlighting Chicago culture I hadn't known. Watching it intensified my desire to live there. I focused on Chicago's infrastructure, lifestyle, and authentic character, and animated the L train routes to bring the city's true essence to life in my delivery.
