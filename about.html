Shema Ndikumana

He/Him

An award-winning multimedia designer with over three years of versatile experience across education, retail, startups, and local government. I've contributed to projects for Baltimore City, the University of Baltimore, Salem Massachusetts, and emerging food businesses and startups in the community.

Rooted in a multicultural background, I approach each project with a broad global perspective, valuing clean design and seeking to elevate the stories of the communities I work with. Whether developing visual identities, capturing compelling photography and videos, or building user-friendly websites with HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, my aim is to craft work that is meaningful and human-centered.

Fueled by a genuine passion for empowering others and celebrating everyday moments, I believe in creating with clarity, heart, and purpose. Let’s work together to turn your vision into something impactful and authentic.
